# Pricing Engine

## Authority Engine tiers — standard pricing

These are the default tier structures. The user may override any pricing — always defer to what they specify. These defaults exist so proposals can be generated quickly when the user says something like "standard pricing" or "our usual tiers."

### Authority Lite — RETIRED

**RETIRED. Do not quote on new business.** Replaced by the **Authority Launchpad** ($2,500 one-time, see below). The tier is kept on record so historical proposals and existing Lite clients still make sense — never present it as a current option, never include it in a tier comparison, never price it.

- **Monthly:** $1,750
- **Commitment:** 3-month minimum
- **Video shoots:** 1/month
- **Short-form pieces:** 4–6
- **Brand photo session:** No (frame grabs from video only)
- **Edited photos:** 5–8
- **Ad management:** Yes (ad spend separate, $500/mo minimum)
- **CRM + automations:** No — client handles their own
- **Lead dashboard:** No
- **Strategy calls:** Monthly
- **Who it's for:** Business owners who need to start building authority and generating leads now but want to ease in. They're willing to handle their own follow-up and CRM. The friction of doing that manually is what drives the upgrade conversation.

## Custom pricing logic

When the user specifies a different price range, scale deliverables proportionally:

- **Below $1,500/mo:** Not recommended. If the user insists, strip to bare minimum: 1 shoot/month, 3–4 short-form pieces, basic ad management, monthly call. No photography, no CRM.
- **$1,500–$2,000/mo range:** No live monthly production tier sits here — the retainer floor is the Organic Authority Engine at $3,000/mo, and Lite is retired. Do not invent a tier to fill the gap. The honest answer below the floor is the **Authority Launchpad** ($2,500 one-time): the foundation gets built once, they own it outright, and there is no retainer they can't carry. If they specifically need an ongoing monthly at this number, the fit is Cutting Room Floor (below), not a stripped-down Authority tier.
- **$2,000–$3,000/mo range:** Bridging zone. At $3,000 this *is* the Organic Authority Engine — quote it as it stands rather than assembling a custom scope. Below $3,000, the choice is the Launchpad (one-time) or a reduced-volume organic scope (6–8 short-form pieces, quarterly photo session, no CRM, no ads). A reduced scope is a custom quote, never presented under a tier name.
- **$3,000–$4,500/mo range:** Maps to Engine tier. Scale content volume up at the higher end.
- **$4,500–$6,000/mo range:** Maps to System tier. Full infrastructure.
- **$6,000+/mo:** Custom scope. Multiple shoots per month, dedicated account management, custom show/series production, or expanded CRM and automation work. Discuss with the user.

## Cutting Room Floor (CRF) tiers

If the prospect is better suited for editing/repurposing services rather than full production:

- **Starter:** $1,250/mo (annual: $1,000/mo — save 20%)
- **Growth:** $2,400/mo (annual: $1,920/mo — save 20%)
- **Bespoke:** $3,500+/mo (custom scope)

CRF is positioned as the lowest-friction entry point into the Churlish ecosystem. Every CRF client is a candidate for full production. Proposals for CRF clients should plant the seed that the Authority Engine is the next step.

## Pricing presentation rules

1. On the monthly tiers, always show monthly investment, not total contract value. "$3,000/month" is easier to process than "$12,000 over 4 months." One-time offers (Authority Launchpad, Revenue X-Ray) show the one-time price and no term.
2. On the ads-managed tiers only (Authority Engine, Authority System), ad spend is ALWAYS noted as separate — never bundle it into the tier price. **Never attach the ad-spend note to the Organic Authority Engine or the Authority Launchpad:** neither runs ads and neither requires any ad budget, so the rider quotes the client money they will never spend.
3. When presenting two or three tiers side by side, the contrast does the selling. The lower tier shows what they'd be missing. The higher tier shows full commitment. The middle tier (when three are presented) becomes the natural choice.
4. Never discount the first month. Never offer "first month free." The pricing reflects the value; undermining it on day one undermines the relationship.
5. If the user asks you to include a discount or incentive, frame it as a commitment reward (e.g., "annual commitment rate") rather than a markdown.

## The Crucible (fractional CMO engagements)

For prospects who need strategic leadership, not just content production — typically positioned through The Crucible (the 90-day embedded implementation sprint run by Brandon and Kelly Bromley):

- **Revenue X-Ray:** $3,500 (diagnostic — credited toward engagement)
- **Crucible Core:** $5,000–$8,000/mo (6-month minimum)
- **Performance Partnership:** Standard fee + 20–25% of revenue growth attributed to the engagement

Only include Crucible pricing if the user specifically asks for it. These are positioned as a separate product from the Authority Engine tiers.
