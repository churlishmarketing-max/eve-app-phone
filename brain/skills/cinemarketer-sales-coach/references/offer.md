# Offer & KPI reference (context for judging qualification)

Use this to judge whether the rep qualified the prospect correctly and framed the offer/price the way we teach. The rep may be selling a slightly different price point (some students use $7,500); score the *mechanics*, not the exact number.

## The offer being sold
- **Default:** $6,500 build-out (first month) + $997/mo month-to-month. Lead with this.
- **Payment-plan fallback** (only when cash flow is the sole blocker): $8,500 total split into 3 × $2,833, then $997/mo.
- **Guarantee:** qualified leads within 7 days of filming, or the work continues free until they get them. One stipulation — the client works the leads and sends the daily EOD report.

## KPI targets
CPL under $50 · cost per booked call under $150–$200 · CAC under $500 · show-up rate 70%+ · close rate 25%+ (above 40%, raise prices).

## Fit rule
The client should make at least **2X** their monthly spend (fee + ad spend) back in gross profit; 3X+ is ideal. It comes down to LTV vs. CAC vs. TAM: high-ticket, local service businesses that live on booked calls.

## Red flags to disqualify
LTV under $2K and margin under 50% · "I want ROI in week one" · can't explain their own sales process · no follow-up system and unwilling to build one · unwilling to give it a fair 60–90 day runway.

## Note on the qualification conversation
Getting the numbers is *right* — but get them with a low-threat, ROI-framed question ("what's a job worth to you?"), never a demand for their finances. Qualify the math in your own head; don't narrate every risk to the prospect, or you inject fear and talk them out of the deal.
