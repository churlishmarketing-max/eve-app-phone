# FORMATS — Deliverable Anatomies

Every deliverable type in the writer's world, reverse-engineered from the archive. Read the matching anatomy before producing; match the archive's conventions unless Brandon overrides. PACKAGE mode lives here.

## 1 · Coded RP post (the house wrapper)

The archive's signature: clean prose injected into a styled HTML shell for the RP platform. Anatomy of the standard shell (per New_Wolf_Template / Trinity Noir):

- `<center><div>` container ~420px, solid border (11px), dark palette (#333/#555/#666 family for Wolf-class posts; palettes vary by character — check the character's existing posts).
- **Header block:** character image (imgur-hosted, ~100px, left-aligned, bordered) · series title right-aligned in display type with a `▷` glyph (e.g., `▷ TRINITY NOIR.`) · 1px divider rule · **tagline** in letter-spaced small caps (*"Unexpected Visitor." · "Be Like Fisher." · "You Don't Know Jack."*).
- **Body block:** justified text, serif (garamond/times family), opens with a giant **drop cap** (`font-size:60px`, batangche), `&nbsp;`-indented first line.
- **Slug lines** before scene text: universe/city/location/time, separated by `<br>`, with `<font size=5>` on the location jump lines (e.g., `Trincity / Wolf's Office / Forty-Eight Hours Ago....`).
- **Color-coded dialogue:** each speaker owns a hex. Established: Wolf `grey`, Bianca `#0099ff`. New characters get a color assigned once and kept forever (log in the canon ledger). Wrapped as `<font color=X>"line"</font color=X>` — the archive's closing-tag style; match it, don't "fix" it, the platform parses it.
- *Italics* for emphasis/inner voice, `<b>` for instructional emphasis in menus.

**Workflow law:** write the prose clean first, get it approved, *then* inject into the shell. Always deliver both versions — coded for posting, plain for the record.

## 2 · RP narrative post / reply (the prose inside)

- **Length:** ~300–1,400 words. Openers run long; mid-thread replies run tighter.
- **Opening moves** (pick per voice-bible): philosophy hook (*"I like to believe that the universe is just a series of events…"*), weather overture, or cold-open-at-the-crisis followed by a rewind slug.
- **Middle:** dialogue-forward; the second voice (Bianca-class) carries exposition as banter; tactical numbers for competence texture.
- **Close:** ALWAYS on a door the partner can walk through — an arrival, a question, a trigger pulled, a name spoken. A reply that closes every loop kills the thread; that's a defect.
- **Reply etiquette:** react to the partner's material specifically before advancing; never control the partner's character (no god-moding); leave their exits open.

## 3 · Prompt menu (the Ares Trials pattern)

1. **In-voice framing intro** — the character explains the premise in their own register (Ares: the Trials of War).
2. **Mechanic paragraph** — how prompts work: situational, affecting the partner's character *directly or indirectly* ("your character is the person of interest, or the person of interest interacts with your character").
3. **Logistics line, bolded** — how to respond: "Comment below with the prompt number and where you would like it sent."
4. **Numbered prompts** — zero-padded (`001.`), each: **bold title with a blade** ("Break a righteous man's faith" · "Best Served Dead") + a 2–5 sentence in-voice pitch that gives the partner room to plot.
5. Ten is the house count; every pitch passes the test *"does this make the other writer's next move obvious and fun?"*

## 4 · Character bio

- **Header:** name + epithet (*Evelyn Wayne — She-Wolf*), then an alliterative tagline in caps (*SENSUAL, SEDUCTIVE, SINISTER.*).
- **Mythic opening** (third-person legend cadence, per voice-bible §7) → **origin arc** in 4–6 escalating paragraphs, each ending on a turn (Wolf bio: enlistment → codename → shadow work → Project VII → betrayal → exile → pirates → the ship Olympus).
- **Stat block:** Name · Birth/True Name · Age · Physical Description (specific, sensory, camera-ready) · Occupation · Personality Type (public face *and* the crack in it — "suffers from RBF") · Skills · Daily Activity · Additional Information/links.
- Secrets are *seeded* in the bio, not spent — the bio promises; the threads pay off.

## 5 · Audio drama script (the Guardians pattern)

- **Location slug** at top (*Nova Police Station*).
- **SFX/action cues bolded in parentheses**, standalone or trailing a line: `**(Footsteps)**` · `**(Slams hands on table)**`.
- **Dialogue** as prose quotes with the speaker tag in bold parens at the end of the block: `"Jaywalking." **(Peter)**` — physical business cues ride in the same trailing parens.
- **Pronunciation guides** inline on first use for invented names: `Kr'nch (Kr-yn-ch)`.
- Every scene passes the radio test (Pendleton-Thompson): would a listener with no picture track *who's talking, where they are, what just happened*? Sound carries the blocking.
- Computer/system voices in caps-styled lines (`Computer Voice: ACCESS ROOM 9`).

## 6 · Novel chapter (publication register)

- **POV:** first person (Prima Facie-class) or close third; one head per scene.
- **Open** in motion — last possible moment (McKinnon), often with the wit-and-dread double register.
- **Run** on a scene clock (Tide Law 5): something expires in every scene.
- **Close** on a turn, never at rest (Koontz law). 1,500–4,000 words is the working band.
- Line standards: publication register per voice-bible — filters cut, orbs retired, fragments rationed to the turns.

## 7 · Bibles, beat sheets & architecture docs (CINEFLUX house style)

Per the FACTORY SETTINGS bible precedent, delivered as clean markdown files:
- **Identity block** (who/what in one breath) → **engine** (the experimental variable / thesis / inversion the piece runs on) → **physical & voice anchors** (specific, generation-ready: the beauty mark, the fixed golden hour, the verbal tics) → **world rules** → **phase dials** (escalation stages, e.g., the four-phase corruption dial) → **mechanics** (watermarks, glitch logic) → **workflow blocks** (paste-ready anchors, prompt packs).
- Beat sheets carry the thesis line explicitly (the Zeke & Selena precedent: *marriage requires ongoing intentionality*) and mark where form reinforces content — the coherence test from the record.
- Every architecture doc ends with open threads + "The One Thing."

## 8 · Comment / interaction posts

Short in-character comment templates (Starlord_Comments pattern): 1–3 beats, full personality, built for thread velocity — a jab, a hook, an opening. Keep a character's comment voice consistent with their long-form posts; comments are the character at conversational speed, not a different character.

## 9 · Image-prompt blocks (Higgsfield / Soul workflow)

For character visuals: **character anchor block** (locked identity details, verbatim-stable across generations) + **per-shot block** (scene, wardrobe, lighting, framing). Follow the FACTORY SETTINGS conventions in the project record; anchors never drift without a canon-ledger entry — visual details are narrative mechanics in this house (the beauty mark is a *plot device*).
