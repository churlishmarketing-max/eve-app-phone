## CLIENT: Rustic Lumber Store / Woodaddy
## AVATAR: The $30K Craftsman

### Demographics
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Age range | 28–55 |
| Gender | 90% male |
| Location | Midwest US (primary), national for DTC |
| Income / revenue | $20K–$100K annual furniture/craft sales |
| Business size | Solo or 1–2 person shop |
| Industry | Woodworking, custom furniture, artisan crafts |
| Experience level | 2–15 years |

### Pain points
1. "I'm barely clearing enough to call this a business after materials and shop costs"
2. "I've been using [Rubio Monocoat / Odie's Oil] because that's what everyone on YouTube uses, but I've never questioned the cost"
3. "My margins are so thin I can't raise my prices without losing customers"
4. "I don't know what my finish is actually costing me per piece"
5. "I feel like I'm competing against IKEA on price and against master craftsmen on quality — stuck in the middle"

### Language patterns
- "That's not a business — that's a really expensive hobby you haven't figured out how to quit yet"
- "I can't afford to experiment with a new finish and risk ruining a piece"
- "Everyone uses [brand name] — why would I switch?"
- "If it ain't broke don't fix it" (the status quo defense)
- "Five bucks? What have I got to lose?"

### Objections
1. **"I've always used [leading brand]"** → Loyalty as identity, not as logic. Handle by showing the math.
2. **"What if it doesn't look as good?"** → Risk aversion on visible results. Handle with $5 sample — zero risk.
3. **"I don't do enough volume for the savings to matter"** → Handle by showing per-piece math, not annual totals.

## AVATAR: The Omaha Homeowner

### Demographics
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Age range | 35–65 |
| Gender | 55% male, 45% female (often couples deciding together) |
| Location | Omaha metro and surrounding areas |
| Income / revenue | $75K–$200K household income |
| Business size | N/A — residential consumer |
| Industry | Homeowner — outdoor living, landscaping, hardscaping |
| Experience level | N/A |

### Pain points
1. "I've had bad experiences with contractors who didn't show up or did shoddy work"
2. "I don't know what a fair price looks like for [outdoor project]"
3. "My backyard has so much potential but I don't know where to start"
4. "I want it done right the first time — I can't afford to redo this"
5. "Every landscaping company looks the same online — how do I pick?"

### Language patterns
- "I want someone I can trust"
- "Show me what you've done before"
- "What's this going to cost me realistically?"
- "My neighbor just got theirs done and it looks amazing"

### Trigger events
1. Spring arrives and they look at the backyard they've been ignoring all winter
2. A neighbor completes a project and it looks great (keeping up with the Joneses)
3. They're hosting a summer event and the outdoor space is embarrassing
4. Home value consideration — they're thinking about selling in 2–3 years

| Gender | 60% female |

1. "I don't know what services are available for my family member"
2. "The system is overwhelming and nobody explains anything clearly"
3. "I feel alone in this — nobody understands what we're going through"
4. "I need help but I don't know who to trust"

### Language patterns
- "Where do I even start?"
- "Is this actually free / covered?"
- "I just want my [family member] to have a good life"
- "I've been turned away before"

### Content implications
- Tone must be warm, accessible, and community-focused — NOT the Churlish call-out style
- Content should educate and reassure, not sell
- Video content should center real people and real stories
- Captions need to be clear and jargon-free

## CLIENT: The Crucible (Brandon King + Kelly Bromley)
## AVATAR: The Plateaued Owner

### Demographics
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Age range | 30–55 |
| Gender | Mixed (varies by vertical — sports/fitness skews male, wellness skews female) |
| Location | Omaha/KC metro initially, expanding regionally |
| Income / revenue | $250K–$2M business revenue |
| Business size | 5–30 employees |
| Industry | Sports performance, fitness, wellness, coaching, service-based businesses |
| Experience level | 5–15 years in business |

### Pain points
1. "Revenue has plateaued and I don't know why"
2. "I've tried agencies before and they just burned through my budget"
3. "I know I need marketing but I don't have time to manage another vendor"
4. "My team is great at delivery but nobody knows how to grow the business"
5. "I see competitors growing and I can't figure out what they're doing differently"

### Objections
1. **"We already have an agency"** → Reframe: "Great — we're not an agency. We build the strategy they execute."
2. **"$5,000+/month is a lot"** → Handle with the Revenue X-Ray as an entry point ($3,500, credited toward engagement)
3. **"How do I know this will be different?"** → Handle with the 90-day structure — defined milestones, permanent assets, measurable outcomes
4. **"I need to see ROI fast"** → Handle with the Performance Partnership model (20–25% of growth)

### Language patterns
- "I'm working harder than ever and the numbers aren't moving"
- "I don't need more marketing — I need a strategy that actually works"
- "Every agency promises the same thing"
- "I can't afford to waste another six months figuring this out"
- "Just tell me what to do and I'll do it" (they want clarity, not options)
