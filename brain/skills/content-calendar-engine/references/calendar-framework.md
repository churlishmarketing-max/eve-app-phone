# Calendar Framework

## The Churlish funnel mix

Every content calendar follows a deliberate ratio of top-of-funnel (TOF), middle-of-funnel (MOF), and bottom-of-funnel (BOF) content. The ratio varies slightly by client stage, but the default is:

### Standard mix: 40/35/25

- **40% TOF (Awareness)** — Content designed to reach new people. Broad appeal, shareable, scroll-stopping. The viewer doesn't know the brand yet — this content introduces them.
- **35% MOF (Consideration)** — Content that builds trust with people who already know the brand. Proof, expertise, deeper value. The viewer is evaluating whether to take the next step.
- **25% BOF (Conversion)** — Content that asks for the action. Offers, testimonials, urgency, direct CTAs. The viewer is ready — this content gives them the push.

### Adjusted mix by client stage:

**New client (first 60 days):** 50/30/20
- Heavier TOF because they need audience first. You can't convert people who don't know you exist.

**Established client (60+ days, steady engagement):** 40/35/25
- Standard mix. Balanced growth.

**Launch or promotion period:** 30/30/40
- Flip to BOF-heavy when there's a specific offer, event, or launch to promote. The audience already exists — now activate it.

**Authority-building phase (no immediate offer):** 45/40/15
- When the goal is pure positioning with no active product/offer to push. Heavy on expertise and proof content.

## Posting cadence by platform

These are default rhythms. The user may override any of these — always defer to what they specify.

### Instagram
- **Feed posts:** 3–5 per week
- **Reels:** 3–5 per week (can overlap with feed posts if video-first)
- **Stories:** Daily (5–7 per week minimum)
- **Priority content types:** Reels (highest reach), Carousels (highest saves), Single image (lowest priority)

### Facebook
- **Feed posts:** 3–5 per week
- **Reels:** 2–3 per week
- **Stories:** 3–5 per week
- **Notes:** Facebook rewards video heavily. Link posts get suppressed. Native video and Reels outperform everything else.

### YouTube
- **Long-form:** 1 per week (or 2 per month minimum)
- **Shorts:** 3–5 per week
- **Notes:** Consistency matters more than volume. One video per week is better than four videos one week and none the next three.

### TikTok
- **Posts:** 5–7 per week (volume platform — more is better here)
- **Notes:** Native feel, trending sounds when relevant, UGC style outperforms polished content

### LinkedIn
- **Posts:** 2–3 per week
- **Notes:** Text-first posts perform well. Video is underutilized on LinkedIn — a Churlish client posting video here stands out. Personal profile posts outperform company page posts.

### Google Business Profile
- **Posts:** 1–2 per week
- **Notes:** Often overlooked. GBP posts appear in local search results. Use for offers, updates, and photos. Directly impacts local SEO.

## Weekly rhythm template

The default weekly structure (adjust per client):

| Day | Content type | Funnel stage | Notes |
|-----|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Monday | Value/educational post | TOF | Start the week with broad-appeal content |
| Tuesday | Behind-the-scenes or process | MOF | Show the work, build trust |
| Wednesday | Sharp take / contrarian POV | TOF | Challenge conventional wisdom, drive engagement |
| Thursday | Client win / case study | MOF | Proof content — specific results |
| Friday | Offer or CTA post | BOF | End the week with a conversion ask |
| Saturday | Personal / brand story | MOF | Humanize the brand, build connection |
| Sunday | Off or Story-only | — | Rest day or light touch (Stories only) |

This is a starting point, not a rigid schedule. The important thing is that the week contains a mix of funnel stages and content types.

## Story strategy

Stories are separate from feed content. They serve a different purpose — maintaining daily presence and building parasocial intimacy. Story content should feel less produced and more personal.

### Story content types:
- **Daily check-in** — What's happening today, what you're working on
- **Behind-the-scenes** — The stuff that doesn't make the feed (setup, bloopers, real moments)
- **Engagement prompts** — Polls, questions, sliders (these feed the algorithm AND give you audience insight)
- **Content teasers** — Preview of what's coming on the feed or YouTube
- **Reshares** — Share feed posts to Stories with added context or a personal reaction
- **Client shoutouts** — Tag clients, share their wins (they reshare = free reach)

### Story cadence:
- 3–7 story frames per day when active
- At least 1 engagement prompt per day (poll, question, slider)
- At least 1 direct CTA per week in Stories (swipe up, link, DM prompt)

## Content pillar distribution

Content pillars should be roughly evenly distributed across the month with flexibility for current events and timely content.

For a client with 4 pillars posting 4 times per week (16 posts/month):
- Each pillar gets ~4 posts per month
- No pillar gets more than 2 posts in the same week
- The funnel stage tag is independent of the pillar — a "Client Wins" pillar post could be TOF (awareness-level transformation story) or BOF (testimonial with CTA)

## Monthly themes (optional)

Some clients benefit from a monthly theme that ties content together. This is not required but adds cohesion when the content volume is high.

**Example monthly theme structure:**
- **Theme of the month:** One overarching topic or angle (e.g., "The $500K Playbook" for GE Outdoors, "Finishing Fundamentals" for Woodaddy)
- **Week 1:** Introduce the theme (TOF)
- **Week 2:** Deep dive / proof (MOF)
- **Week 3:** Objection handling / FAQ (MOF)
- **Week 4:** Offer or CTA tied to the theme (BOF)

## Batch creation framework

Content calendars are designed to be produced in batches, not one post at a time. The default batch cycle:

**Day 1: Plan**
- Finalize the month's calendar
- Identify which posts require new assets vs. repurposed content
- Script any video content

**Day 2: Produce**
- Shoot day — capture all video and photo content for the month
- Record all short-form hooks and talking points
- Capture B-roll and behind-the-scenes footage

**Day 3: Edit**
- Edit all video content
- Design all graphic/carousel assets
- Write all captions and copy

**Day 4: Schedule**
- Load all content into scheduling tool
- Set publish times per platform
- Queue Stories content for the week

This 4-day cycle can produce a full month of content. Some clients prefer weekly batches (shoot once per week, produce that week's content). Either works — the calendar accommodates both.

## Repurposing framework

Every piece of long-form content should generate multiple short-form pieces. The calendar should account for this cascade:

**1 long-form video (YouTube)** can become:
- 3–5 short-form clips (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)
- 1–2 carousel posts (key takeaways)
- 1 blog post or written summary
- 5–8 Story frames
- 1–3 quote graphics
- 1 email newsletter topic

**1 podcast episode** can become:
- 2–4 audiogram clips
- 3–5 short-form video clips (if recorded on camera)
- 1–2 carousels
- 1 blog post
- Pull quotes for social

**1 photo shoot** can become:
- 10–20 individual post images
- 3–5 carousel sets
- Story content for 1–2 weeks
- Website and ad creative assets
- Google Business Profile photos

When building a calendar for a client with regular production days, map these cascades explicitly so nothing gets wasted.
