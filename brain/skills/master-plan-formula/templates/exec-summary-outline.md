# Executive Summary Outline

The condensed 5-section version of the Master Plan. Same strategic content as the full plan, compressed for fast reading. Typically 1-2 printed pages.

The exec summary is the version the business owner shares with:
- Co-founders or business partners
- Advisors, investors, lenders
- Key team members during alignment
- Themselves in 6 months when re-orienting

It is **not** a different argument. It is the same argument, compressed.

---

## Structure

```
Doc Header
├── Title (matching the full plan title + "Executive Summary" subtitle)
├── Subtitle (mark clearly as Executive Summary)
└── Meta

Section 01 — The Strategic Bet
└── Same paragraph as the full plan — verbatim

Section 02 — The Operating Model
├── 1-sentence framing
└── Compressed pillar list — name + one-line role + price tag
    (No detailed card grid; just a clean list)

Section 03 — Pricing Architecture
└── The comparison table only
    (No individual pricing cards; the compare-table gives the same info compressed)

Section 04 — Revenue Model
├── Cash-hero with the target
└── Metric table with the math (same as full plan)

Section 05 — The Endgame
└── Endgame hero — same vision statement as full plan

Page Footer (with note: "Full Master Plan available — see [filename]")
```

---

## What's cut (and why)

The full plan has 10 sections; the exec summary has 5. What's removed:

| Section dropped | Why it's not in the summary |
|----------------|----------------------------|
| Service Ladder (04 in full) | Implied by the Pricing Architecture; redundant in compressed form |
| KPIs and Tracking (06 in full) | Operational detail; not needed for high-level alignment |
| Gap Analysis (07 in full) | Internal-facing detail; exec summary readers usually don't need the audit |
| Roadmap / Phases (08 in full) | Implied by the Endgame + Revenue Model targets |
| Q&A / Internal Notes (10 in full) | Pre-empts internal questions, not external ones |

The cuts are **operational detail**, not strategic content. The exec summary keeps:
- The strategic position
- The business model
- The pricing
- The math
- The vision

## Length

Target: 1 page (printed) for a tight business, 2 pages max for a complex one.

By section, approximate render length:
- Header: ~5% of page
- 01 — Strategic Bet: 15% of page
- 02 — Operating Model: 20% of page
- 03 — Pricing Architecture: 25% of page (the table is dense)
- 04 — Revenue Model: 20% of page
- 05 — Endgame: 15% of page

If it spills to a third page, cut. The value of the exec summary is in compression.

## File naming convention

```
[Business_Name]_Executive_Summary_v[N].html
[Business_Name]_Executive_Summary_v[N].pdf
[Business_Name]_Executive_Summary_v[N].docx
```

Examples:
- `Churlish_Media_Executive_Summary_v1.pdf`
- `Roof_Tulsa_Executive_Summary_v2.docx`

Version number should match the full plan version — they're produced as a pair.

## Visual treatment notes

The exec summary uses the same visual style as the full plan but with these adjustments:

- **Header includes "Executive Summary" tag** — use a section-tag style in teal-light, so readers immediately understand they're holding the condensed version
- **Page footer notes the full plan availability** — small text at bottom: "Full Master Plan: [Business_Name]_Master_Plan_v[N].pdf"
- **No table of contents** — the document is too short
- **Numbered sections still apply** — use 01-05 numbering, not just headers without numbers, so the structure stays recognizable

The exec summary should feel like a **smaller version of the same document**, not a different document. Same fonts, same palette, same spacing — just less content per section.
