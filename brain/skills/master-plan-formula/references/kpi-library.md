# KPI Library

KPIs organized by business type. Pick 6-12 total for a Master Plan — divided across Revenue, Operations, and Marketing categories. KPIs that aren't reviewed weekly aren't real.

For each KPI: name | how to calculate | typical range | review cadence | priority.

---

## Universal KPIs (apply to almost every business)

### Revenue & Pipeline
- **Total Revenue (annual / monthly)** — Self-explanatory. The headline number.
- **MRR / ARR** — Monthly or Annual Recurring Revenue. Only for subscription/retainer businesses.
- **New Revenue / Month** — Net-new revenue closed in the month. Distinct from MRR.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** — Total revenue / number of orders. For non-subscription businesses.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** — Total acquisition spend / new customers acquired.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** — Average revenue per customer × retention duration.
- **LTV:CAC ratio** — Should be 3:1 minimum for healthy unit economics.

### Operations
- **Delivery Time** — How long from order to fulfillment. Varies wildly by business.
- **Capacity Utilization** — % of available capacity in use. Above 85% means hire; below 60% means sell harder.
- **Rework / Revision Rate** — % of deliverables that require revision. High rate = process gap.

### Marketing & Acquisition
- **Conversion Rate (top of funnel → customer)** — Total customers / total leads. Industry-dependent baseline.
- **Channel Mix** — % of new business by acquisition channel.
- **Lead Volume** — New leads per week/month.

## Service Business KPIs (Pattern A: Retainer)

### Revenue
- **Retainer MRR** — Sum of all active retainer revenue
- **Project Revenue / Month** — Non-recurring revenue from one-off work
- **Client Retention Rate** — % of clients renewing past minimum term
- **Churn Rate** — % of clients ending engagement (inverse of retention)
- **Net Revenue Retention** — (Existing client revenue this period / Existing client revenue last period) × 100

### Operations
- **Average Retainer Length** — How many months clients stay
- **Time per Client / Week** — Hours of delivery per client per week
- **Profit Margin per Client** — (Client revenue - delivery cost) / client revenue

### Marketing
- **Discovery Call → Proposal Rate** — % of calls that result in sent proposals
- **Proposal → Close Rate** — % of proposals that close to clients
- **Days from First Touch to Close** — Sales cycle length
- **Referral % of New Business** — How dependent the business is on referrals (high = fragile)

## Product Business KPIs (Pattern C)

### Revenue
- **Gross Sales / Month**
- **Return Rate** — % of orders returned
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**
- **Repeat Purchase Rate** — % of customers who buy a second time within 12 months
- **Cart Abandonment Rate** — For ecommerce

### Operations
- **Inventory Turnover** — Cost of goods sold / average inventory
- **Days Inventory Outstanding** — Average days an item sits in stock
- **Fulfillment Time** — Order placed → shipped
- **Stockout Rate** — % of SKUs out of stock at any given time

### Marketing
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** — Critical for paid-acquisition product businesses
- **Channel ROAS** (Return on Ad Spend) — Revenue / ad spend, by channel
- **Email List Growth Rate**
- **Repeat Customer % from Email vs Paid**

## SaaS KPIs (Pattern D)

### Revenue
- **MRR / ARR**
- **New MRR / Month**
- **Expansion MRR** — Revenue from existing customer upgrades
- **Contraction MRR** — Revenue lost from downgrades
- **Churn MRR** — Revenue lost from cancellations
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** — Should be >100% for healthy SaaS
- **Annual Contract Value (ACV)** — Average annual revenue per customer

### Operations
- **Active Users (DAU/MAU)**
- **Feature Adoption Rate** — % of users using key features
- **Support Tickets per User** — High = product issues or onboarding gap
- **Time to First Value** — Sign-up → first meaningful action

### Marketing
- **CAC by Channel**
- **CAC Payback Period** — Months to recoup CAC
- **Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate**
- **Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate**

### Revenue

## How to choose which KPIs to track

For the Master Plan, pick **6-12 KPIs total**. More creates dashboard fatigue and nothing gets reviewed. Selection criteria:

1. **Two or three revenue KPIs** — including the headline (annual revenue or MRR)
2. **Two or three operations KPIs** — what determines whether delivery scales
3. **Two or three marketing KPIs** — what determines whether new customers arrive
4. **One or two "leading indicators"** — early signals that predict the lagging numbers

For each KPI in the plan, state:
- **Name**
- **Current value** (or "TBD — instrument first")
- **Target value** with date
- **Review cadence** (weekly / monthly / quarterly)
- **Owner** (who's responsible for reviewing and reporting)

If a KPI doesn't pass all five tests, drop it.

---

## Leading vs lagging indicators

The most common KPI mistake: tracking only **lagging indicators** (revenue, retention, profit) that you can't change today.

Always include 1-2 **leading indicators** — actions that predict future lagging results:

| Lagging | Leading |
|---------|---------|
| Revenue | Discovery calls booked |
| Retention | NPS / qualitative client check-ins |
| Profit margin | Hours per delivery |
| Customer LTV | Onboarding completion rate |
| Conversion rate | Top-of-funnel volume |

The Master Plan KPIs section should pair leading + lagging so the operator can act, not just react.

## Industry benchmark notes

When the brain-dump doesn't include current values, flag with "TBD" and optionally add an industry benchmark for context:

- Service business retention: 70-90% annually is healthy
- SaaS NRR: 110%+ is excellent, 100%+ is healthy
- Ecommerce repeat purchase rate: 20-40% is healthy
- Email open rate: 30-40% is industry standard
- Discovery call → proposal conversion: 50-70% is healthy
- Proposal → close: 30-50% is healthy
- LTV:CAC ratio: 3:1 minimum healthy, 5:1+ is excellent

Cite as "industry benchmark" not "this business" until measured.
