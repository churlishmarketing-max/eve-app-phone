# Pricing Architectures

Pricing patterns by business type. Apply the appropriate template based on what's parsed from the brain-dump. The Churlish default (3-tier service retainer) is the canonical version; everything else adapts.

---

## The universal principles

These hold for every business type:

1. **Three tiers minimum.** Two creates "expensive vs cheap." Three creates a recommended middle. Four+ creates decision paralysis.
2. **Middle tier is the anchor.** Visually dominant, marked POPULAR/RECOMMENDED, price-anchored to be where most should buy.
3. **Minimum commitments are non-negotiable.** Discomfort with commitment IS the filter for fit.
4. **Never discount month one.** Discount month four if needed for retention. Month-one discount trains buyers to wait.
5. **Ad spend / pass-through costs are separate.** Never bundled. State them explicitly.
6. **Each tier solves a different problem.** Not "more of the same thing." A different scope, audience, or commitment level.

## Pattern A: Service-Business Retainer (Churlish default)

**Use when:** Business sells time + expertise on a recurring basis (agency, consultant, professional services, B2B services).

**Structure:**

| Tier | Price | Min Term | What it is |
|------|-------|----------|------------|
| Starter | $1,250–$1,750/mo | 3 months | Light retainer, single-channel, monthly cadence |
| Core (POPULAR) | $2,400–$3,500/mo | 6 months | Full retainer, multi-channel, weekly cadence |
| Premium | $5,000+/mo | 6-12 months | Full retainer + strategic partnership + custom scope |

**Add a paid pre-qualifier:**
- $297–$997 audit, diagnostic, or single-engagement
- Filters tire-kickers
- Credit toward retainer if they upgrade within 30 days

**Add project work as on-ramp:**
- Quick Win: $997 (one-off, narrow scope)
- Sprint: $3,500 (focused 30-day engagement)
- Foundations: $2,500-$4,500/mo (3-month limited engagement before full retainer)

## Pattern B: Subscription / Productized Service

**Use when:** Business sells deliverables on a recurring basis (subscription editing, design subscriptions, content subscriptions).

**Structure:**

| Tier | Price | Min Term | What it is |
|------|-------|----------|------------|
| Starter | $1,000–$1,500/mo | 3 months | Capped deliverables, slower turnaround |
| Growth (POPULAR) | $2,000–$3,000/mo | 6 months | Increased volume, faster turnaround, monthly strategy |
| Bespoke | $3,500+/mo | 6 months | Custom scope, fastest turnaround, dedicated point of contact |

Productized services price by **scope and speed**, not hours. Three levers:
- Volume of deliverables
- Turnaround time
- Number of revisions

## Pattern C: Product Business

**Use when:** Business sells physical or digital products.

**Structure: Good / Better / Best**

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| Good | Entry SKU | Solves the basic problem |
| Better (POPULAR) | Mid SKU | Solves more, better materials/features |
| Best | Premium SKU | Solves all, lifetime warranty/upgrade |

For product businesses, add:
- **Subscription option** for repeat-use products (auto-replenish, "Pro Series" club)
- **Bundle** at higher AOV
- **Annual commitment** for subscription products (e.g., $X/year billed annually saves 17%)

## Pattern D: SaaS

**Use when:** Business sells software access on subscription.

**Structure:**

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| Free / Starter | $0–$29/mo | Usage-capped, no support, single user |
| Pro (POPULAR) | $79–$299/mo | Full features, email support, small team |
| Enterprise | $500+/mo (custom) | SLA, dedicated CSM, custom integration |

For SaaS, add:
- **Annual billing discount** (15-20%)
- **Per-seat pricing** above a threshold
- **Usage-based add-ons** (API calls, storage, etc.)

## Pattern E: Creator / Personal Brand

**Use when:** Business is built around an individual's audience and authority.

**Structure:**

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| Free content | $0 | YouTube, podcast, newsletter |
| Low-ticket digital | $47–$297 | Course, ebook, template pack |
| Community / Membership (POPULAR) | $47–$297/mo | Paid community, monthly content, monthly call |
| High-ticket | $1,500–$10,000+ | Cohort, mastermind, 1:1 coaching |

For creators, add:
- **Sponsorship tiers** (separate revenue stream): Bronze / Silver / Gold by audience exposure
- **Course bundles** at higher AOV
- **Annual community pricing** at 10-12× monthly (slight discount for annual)

## Pattern F: Coaching / Consulting (1:1 or Small Group)

**Use when:** Business is the operator's time + expertise applied to specific clients.

**Structure:**

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| VIP Day / Intensive | $2,500–$10,000 | One-day deep engagement |
| Cohort / Group Program (POPULAR) | $3,000–$10,000 for 8-12 weeks | Small group, fixed curriculum |
| 1:1 Engagement | $5,000–$25,000+/mo or quarter | Direct access, customized work |

For coaching, add:
- **Application required** for the top tier (artificial scarcity is real scarcity here)
- **Cohort timing** (start dates, not always-on)
- **Alumni community** as included or upsell from cohorts

---

## Pattern G: Performance / Revenue-Share

**Use when:** Business ties compensation to client results (sales-as-a-service, growth marketing, performance media).

**Structure:**

| Tier | Setup / Base | Performance Component |
|------|--------------|----------------------|
| Setup + Retainer | $5,000 setup + $3,500/mo | None (pure retainer) |
| Hybrid (POPULAR) | $3,500/mo | + 15% of attributed growth |
| Pure Performance | $0–$1,500/mo | + 20–30% of attributed growth |

For performance pricing, add:
- **Attribution methodology** stated explicitly
- **Floor + ceiling** (minimum hour-equivalent, maximum payout)
- **Quarterly review and renegotiation**
- **Minimum 6 months** so attribution can stabilize

## How to choose the right pattern

Match to the business type parsed from the brain-dump:

| If brain-dump describes... | Use pattern |
|---------------------------|-------------|
| Agency, marketing firm, consulting | A (Service-Business Retainer) |
| Editing service, design subscription, content-as-a-service | B (Subscription) |
| Physical product, ecommerce, retail | C (Product) |
| Software, app, platform | D (SaaS) |
| Audience-led, content creator, personal brand | E (Creator) |
| Coach, course creator, mentor | F (Coaching) |
| Performance marketing, growth-for-equity, sales-as-a-service | G (Performance) |

For **hybrid businesses** (e.g., agency that also sells a course), use Pattern A for the core business and add the secondary pattern as an additional pillar with its own tier structure.

## Naming the tiers

Tier names matter. They're the first impression of the offer. Rules:

- **3 syllables max** — easier to remember
- **Evocative, not generic** — "Engine" beats "Standard," "Bespoke" beats "Premium"
- **Sequential by ambition** — "Starter / Growth / Bespoke" reads as a path. "Bronze / Silver / Gold" reads as commodity.
- **Avoid corporate-speak** — no "Solutions," "Suite," "Platinum," "Enterprise" unless you're actually SaaS

**Good tier name sets:**
- Starter / Growth / Bespoke (CRF style)
- Lite / Engine / System (AE style)
- Foundation / Build / Studio
- Spark / Ignition / Launch (Brandon's Brand/CPG tiers)
- Apprentice / Operator / Owner
- Read / Practice / Master

**Tier names to avoid (overused):**
- Basic / Pro / Premium
- Bronze / Silver / Gold / Platinum
- Standard / Plus / Ultimate
- Small / Medium / Large

## Pricing math sanity check

After setting prices, verify:

1. **Can the operator deliver Tier 2 at scale?** If the popular tier is $2,400/mo and the operator can only deliver to 8 clients, the business caps at $19,200/mo. Verify this matches the revenue target.

2. **Does the price floor cover variable cost?** Tier 1 should still be profitable at full capacity. If Tier 1 is $1,250/mo and variable cost per client is $1,000, the margin is too thin.

3. **Does the price ladder make sense?** Tier 2 should be 1.5-2.5× Tier 1. Tier 3 should be 1.5-2× Tier 2. Bigger jumps confuse buyers; smaller jumps don't differentiate.

4. **Is the minimum commitment defensible?** A 6-month minimum is defensible if the work compounds. A 6-month minimum on a one-shot deliverable is hostile.
