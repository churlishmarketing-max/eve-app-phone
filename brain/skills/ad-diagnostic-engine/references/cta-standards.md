# CTA Standards

The Churlish Media CTA approval list. Applies to **all ad work** — Churlish-internal and every client. Auto-replace banned weak CTAs with approved patterns. Enforce regardless of what the client requests; explain the swap.

---

## The four required CTA properties

Every approved CTA carries all four:

1. **Benefit-led** — Names what they GET, not what they DO
2. **Urgency-loaded** — Gives a reason to act now (scarcity, deadline, qualification)
3. **Qualifier-baked** — Implies who this is for (filters bad-fit clicks)
4. **Effort-light** — Sounds easy and valuable, not like work

Missing any of the four = the CTA is weaker than it should be.

---

## Banned weak CTAs

These ship in roughly 90% of bad direct-response ads. **Never use them.** They violate at least two of the four required properties (usually all four).

- "Book a call with me"
- "Schedule a call"
- "Learn more"
- "Click here"
- "Click here to book"
- "Want to chat?"
- "Let's talk"
- "Hop on a call"
- "Call me to learn more"
- "Get in touch"
- "Contact us today"
- "Sign up"

**Why they fail:**
- Frame the CTA as *work for the prospect* (booking, scheduling, calling)
- Provide zero benefit signal
- No urgency, no qualifier
- Sound like every other ad — pattern-match into ignore

## Approved CTA patterns

Each one carries all four required properties. Use as-is, customize the bracketed variable, or use as templates for net-new CTAs in the same structure.

### Strategy / Discovery focus
- **Let's map out your game plan — grab a free call today.**
- **Apply now and see if you qualify for a free strategy session.**
- **Get a custom [solution] plan — book your free call now.**
- **See exactly how we'll [fix problem] — apply for a quick call.**

### Pain-led
- **Struggling with [pain point]? Let's solve it in 15 minutes.**
- **Tired of [pain]? Book a 15-min fix call.**
- **[Pain] keeping you stuck? Get a free [solution] plan.**

### Scarcity-led
- **Claim your free [benefit] session — limited spots available.**
- **Only [N] spots open this month — apply now.**
- **Free [benefit] session — first [N] only.**

### Qualifier-led
- **Apply now — we only take [criteria] this quarter.**
- **See if you qualify for [program]: 2-min application.**
- **Built for [specific avatar]. Apply to see if you fit.**

### Outcome-led
- **Book a fast, no-pressure chat to [get specific outcome].**
- **15-min call → custom [outcome] roadmap.**
- **Get your [specific deliverable] in [timeframe].**

## CTA review process

When reviewing any ad, landing page, or email CTA:

1. **Identify the CTA** — pull it out of the surrounding copy
2. **Score against the four properties** — Benefit / Urgency / Qualifier / Effort
3. **Check against the banned list** — auto-flag if found
4. **If failing on any axis, rewrite** — use approved patterns or build a new one in the same structure
5. **Present before/after** — explain why the original failed and what the new version improves

---

## Hard rules

- **Never ship a banned CTA**, even if the client signed off on it. Explain the swap.
- **One CTA per ad.** Never two. Never three. The brain freezes on choice; pick the strongest.
- **The CTA must match the offer.** "Book a free call" with no offer behind it = wasted CTA. Make the value of the call concrete.
- **The CTA appears at the end of the ad and in the headline/copy.** Repeat for retention; vary the phrasing.
- **Test 2–3 CTA variants per campaign.** Even within the approved patterns, different audiences respond to different framings.
