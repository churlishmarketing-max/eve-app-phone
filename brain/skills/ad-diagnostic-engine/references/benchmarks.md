# Ad Performance Benchmarks

All thresholds calibrated for **local high-ticket service businesses running Meta ads**. Transferable to other paid social platforms with minor adjustment. Source: Internal Churlish Media benchmarking standards.

---

## 1. Cost Per Lead (CPL)

**Formula:** CPL = Amount Spent ÷ Number of Leads

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| $0–$30 | Great ✅ |
| $31–$50 | Good 👍 |
| $51–$80 | Poor ⚠️ |
| $81+ | Bad ❌ |

**Diagnosis flow if high:** CTR → Hook-to-Lead Efficiency → Opt-in Rate → CPC → CPM. CPL under $50 is solid. Over $80 = losing money — fix offer, audience, or creative.

---

## 2. Hook-to-Lead Efficiency

**Formula:** (Leads ÷ 3-Second Video Plays) × 100

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 50%+ | Exceptional ✅ |
| 40–49% | Excellent 👍 (sweet spot) |
| 30–39% | Good ⚡ |
| 20–29% | Fair ⚠️ |
| <20% | Poor ❌ |

**The most important metric in the system.** Diagnoses whether the hook is doing its real job — converting attention into action.

---

## 3. Video Hook Rate

**Formula:** (3-Second Video Plays ÷ Impressions)

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 30%+ | Excellent ✅ |
| 20–29% | Good 👍 |
| 10–19% | Fair ⚠️ |
| <10% | Poor ❌ |

**Common fixes:** Bold opener, pattern interrupt in first second, high-contrast visuals, faster pacing, refresh creative if fatigued.

---

## 4. Link CTR

**Formula:** (Link Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1.2%+ | Excellent ✅ |
| 0.8–1.19% | Good 👍 |
| 0.5–0.79% | Fair ⚠️ |
| <0.5% | Poor ❌ |

**Diagnosis flow if low:** Offer strength → creative engagement → CTA strength → audience quality → ad placements.

---

## 5. CTR (All)

**Formula:** (Total Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100

Includes link clicks + engagement clicks (likes, shares, comments, expand) + other clicks.

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 2.5%+ | Excellent ✅ |
| 1.5–2.49% | Good 👍 |
| 1.0–1.49% | Fair ⚠️ |
| <1.0% | Poor ❌ |

**Reads pair with Link CTR** — high CTR (All) + low Link CTR = engagement without intent. Fix CTA. Both low = ad ignored. Rebuild.

---

## 6. Opt-in Rate

**Formula:** (Leads ÷ Link Clicks)

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 20%+ | Excellent ✅ |
| 10–19% | Good 👍 |
| 5–9% | Fair ⚠️ |
| <5% | Poor ❌ |

**Common fixes:** Stronger offer, fewer form fields (3–5 max), clearer CTA, faster mobile-friendly landing page, trust signals.

---

## 7. CPC – Link

**Formula:** Total Spent ÷ Number of Link Clicks

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| ≤$1.50 | Excellent ✅ |
| $1.51–$3.00 | Good 👍 |
| $3.01–$5.00 | Fair ⚠️ |
| >$5.00 | Poor ❌ |

**Common causes of high CPC-Link:** Weak hook, broad targeting, low ad relevance score, weak CTA, competitive market, inefficient placements.

---

## 8. CPC – All

**Formula:** Total Spent ÷ Total Clicks

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| ≤$0.50 | Excellent ✅ |
| $0.51–$1.50 | Good 👍 |
| $1.51–$3.00 | Fair ⚠️ |
| >$3.00 | Poor ❌ |

**Reads pair with CPC – Link** — high CPC (All) + low CPC (Link) = engagement without conversion. Strengthen CTA. Both high = full creative + targeting refresh.

---

## 9. CPM

**Formula:** (Total Spent ÷ Impressions) × 1,000

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| <$10 | Excellent ✅ |
| $10–$20 | Good 👍 |
| $20–$40 | Fair ⚠️ |
| >$40 | Poor ❌ |

**Common causes of high CPM:** Audience too narrow, low ad relevance score, poor engagement, seasonal competition, market saturation.

---

## 10. Frequency

**Formula:** Impressions ÷ Reach

| Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Optimal ✅ |
| 2–3 | Caution Zone ⚠️ |
| 3–5 | High Risk ❌ |
| 5+ | Ad Fatigue 🚨 |

**Automatic action:** Frequency 3+ triggers a creative-refresh recommendation regardless of other metrics. Refresh creatives every 2–3 weeks as standard practice. Exception: retargeting campaigns where higher frequency is expected.

---

## Quick reference: which metric to look at first

| Symptom | Look at first | Then check |
|---|---|---|
| CPL too high | CTR | Hook-to-Lead → Opt-in → CPC |
| Low conversions | Hook-to-Lead Efficiency | Opt-in Rate |
| Low engagement | Video Hook Rate | CTR (All) |
| High CPC | CPM | Audience size + ad relevance |
| Performance dropping over time | Frequency | Creative age |
| Lots of clicks, no leads | Opt-in Rate | Landing page + offer |
| Lots of views, no clicks | Link CTR | CTA + offer clarity |
