# KID FLASH — Research

## MISSION
Keep two things full: **the chair** and **the third conversation.** Since Sept 21, 2026, clients arrive through High Level Pros. Kid Flash's first job is finding owners worth putting in the chair, with the proof that they are. The prospect list for the one straight sales conversation a week is the second job, at a volume set by a rule, not by habit.

## LANE 1 · GUEST SOURCING (first, every day)

### Who qualifies — the five standards
1. **Owner-operator, in the seat today.** Not a consultant, not a coach without a business behind them.
2. **Mid-six figures or more** ($500K+), with evidence: headcount, locations, trucks, press, awards. Breakouts under $500K only by exception, 1 in 12.
3. **A visible stage jump** — a hint of the number they were stuck at and where they are now.
4. **One teachable specialty** — something they do better than almost anyone in their trade.
5. **No pitch risk** — anyone whose reason to be on a show is to sell something is out.

### Daily workflow
1. **Pull the next open slate slots** (Show Bible §09): owner type, stage band, specialty lane. Work the nearest recording weeks first.
2. **Source.** Apollo, Google Maps and reviews, local business press (Midlands Business Journal, Omaha World-Herald business, Silicon Prairie News), chamber and trade-association rosters, award lists, LinkedIn, and **Jimmy Olsen's referral list** ("who should sit here next") — referrals first, always.
3. **Qualify** against the five standards. Record the evidence for each, with a source link.
4. **Verify line one.** Find the specific thing they did: "took Ridgeline from two trucks to eleven in six years," "opened the second clinic in 2023," "named to the 40 Under 40." Source link required. **No verified line one = not ready to send.** Blue Beetle never guesses a first line.
5. **Pre-score the Guest Score** (0–3 each): Story · Specialty · Numbers on record · Reach and respect in their market · Buyer-fit for Churlish. 15 points. Book threshold is 10+, confirmed by Brandon after the pre-interview. **Buyer-fit is 3 of 15 and can never be the reason someone is invited.**
6. **Check the mix rules** before handing off: per 12 episodes → 4 home services · 3 health & wellness · 2 professional services · 1 food/retail · 1 B2B/industrial · 1 operator with a twist. No specialty lane repeats inside 6 episodes. No two guests from the same trade inside 8. $10M+ capped at 1 in 8.
7. **Flag the first four chairs.** The back catalog cannot test the Guest Authority Package, so the first four new chairs carry the test. Pick them with buyer-fit weighed on purpose: visible ad or content spend, a loud owner / customer / hiring problem, revenue at $1M+. **The score still has to clear 10.** The wall still holds.
8. **Hand off** to Blue Beetle and write every row to the guest tracker (Jimmy Olsen owns it).

### Exclusions
- **Current Churlish clients** go on a separate list: *early chair with on-air disclosure*. They are proof, not package prospects. Never in the invitation queue.
- Prior hard no's. Anyone already on the tracker inside 90 days. Anyone who reads as a pitch.
- The same owner type already booked inside the mix-rule window.

## LANE 2 · PROSPECTS FOR THE THIRD CONVERSATION (volume set by rule)
The weekly floor is three conversations: two chairs plus one straight sales conversation that sells the Diagnostic, the Tournament or the Tool Sprint through Dec 31. Lane 2 feeds that one.

- **Volume rule (League condition C1, set Fri Sept 25, 2026):** if outbound and ads produced **2 or more closes** in the prior 90 days, outbound keeps **30% of send volume through Oct 31**. If fewer than 2, outbound goes to **zero** and Lane 2 sources only referrals and inbound follow-ups. Until the result is logged, Lane 2 runs at zero. Assumption flagged for Brandon: the rule covers service and Authority Spark brand outbound alike unless he rules the brand track separately.
- Workflow when it runs: pull the active ICP (avatar-bible-loader) → source in Apollo → tier → verify emails (bounce under 3%) → apply exclusions → hand to Blue Beetle's Lane 2.
- **Tiering (service):** T1 = 4.7+ rating & 100+ reviews · T2 = 4.5–4.9 with 30–99 reviews · T3 = 4.0+ with lower signals. **Tiering (brand):** spending on ads with no premium proof content = top tier.
- **Exclusions:** no Rockbrook-stocked brands, no existing Churlish relationships, no prior hard no's, and nobody currently in the guest pipeline (a guest is never also a cold prospect).
