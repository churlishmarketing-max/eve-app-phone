## THE PROTOCOL

### 1. Baseline sweep — measure before touching anything

Run the standard 10-query battery (see `references/query-battery.md`) against the answer engines. For each query record three things: **mentioned or absent · position/framing · which source the engine leaned on.** The output is a share-of-answer number: "mentioned in 2 of 10 queries" — that's the baseline every future month gets measured against. Numbers over adjectives; "low AI visibility" is not a finding, "2/10, never first, sourced from a 2019 directory listing" is.

### 2. Source audit — find what the engines are eating

AI answers are assembled from a knowable diet. Audit the client against each source, because these are the levers:

- **Entity hygiene** — consistent name/address/phone everywhere, schema.org markup (LocalBusiness / Service / FAQPage), an About page that states in plain sentences who they are, what they do, where, and for whom. Engines can't recommend an entity they can't resolve.
- **Google Business Profile + reviews** — volume, velocity, recency, and whether reviews contain the service keywords in natural language.
- **Community presence** — Reddit, local Facebook groups, niche forums. Engines weight "real people recommending" heavily. Never fake this (Law 9 again) — the play is earning and prompting real mentions.
- **YouTube transcripts** — Churlish's unfair advantage. The engines read transcripts. Every client video the studio already produces is AEO fuel if the metadata and spoken content name the entity, the service, and the geography. This is the moat: competitors selling AEO can't manufacture a video library; Churlish's clients already have one.
- **The client's own site** — answer-shaped content: FAQ pages that mirror the query battery verbatim, honest "[service] in [city] — how to choose" comparison content, plain-language service pages.
- **Press and directories** — local media mentions, chamber listings, industry directories the engines actually cite (record which ones showed up in the baseline sweep — cite what the machine cited).

### 3. The fix stack — ranked by effort against impact

Ship as a ranked list, quick wins first: (1) entity hygiene and schema — hours, foundational; (2) FAQ/answer-shaped pages mirroring the battery — days; (3) YouTube metadata + transcript pass on existing library — hand to `youtube-metadata`; (4) review velocity program; (5) community/press citations — slowest, compounds hardest. Every item carries effort, owner, and expected source it influences.

### 4. Re-measure monthly

Same battery, same engines, same recording format. The deliverable line that sells renewals: "mentioned in 2/10 in April → 6/10 in July, first-position twice." Log every measurement — the accumulating ledger is both the client report and the Churlish sales proof.
