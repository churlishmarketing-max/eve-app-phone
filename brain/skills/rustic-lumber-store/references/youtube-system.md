# Rustic Lumber Store — YouTube Metadata System

## How to Generate YouTube Metadata for RLS

When asked to create YouTube titles, thumbnails, descriptions, or tags for RLS content, follow this exact system. It integrates the Jay Alto Thumbnail Framework, the Title Generator V2 click triggers, the Top 10 CTR Elements, and RLS brand rules.

---

## Step 1: Analyze the Transcript

Before writing anything, identify:
1. **The core story/conflict** — What's the emotional hook? (e.g., calling out competitors, origin story, price comparison)
2. **The money quotes** — Verbatim lines from Zach that are inherently clickable
3. **The visual moments** — Scenes that would make compelling thumbnails (transformations, shop tours, before/after)
4. **The audience** — Which of the 3 personas (hobbyist, pro, DIY renovator) is this primarily for?

---

## Step 2: Generate Thumbnail Text Options

### Rules (from Jay Alto + Thumbnail Text AI Prompt):
- **1-4 words maximum** (3 words or less is ideal)
- Text must **add curiosity, NOT give the answer**
- Challenge an assumption the viewer has
- Slightly controversial is good
- Don't repeat the title — thumbnail and title work as a PAIR

### Thumbnail Framework (Attention + Trust + Curiosity):

**Attention:** What stops the scroll?
- Dramatic image (before/after transformation, shop environment, product comparison)
- Bold color contrast (price in green vs. red, product vs. competitor)
- Zach's face showing genuine emotion (frustration, pride, "let me explain")

**Trust:** What makes them believe it's worth watching?
- Real workshop environment (sawdust, tools, tables in progress)
- Product-hacking recognizable brands (Rubio can visible or implied)
- Zach as a real person, not a polished spokesperson

**Curiosity:** What makes them HAVE to click?
- Pose a question/puzzle ("SAME PRODUCT?" — are they really the same?)
- Violation of expectation ("$30 PROFIT." — no brand admits this)
- Missing information ("WHY SO CHEAP?" — the viewer must click to find out)

### Generate 5 thumbnail text options:
- 2 options at 1-2 words (maximum scroll-stopping power)
- 2 options at 3 words
- 1 option at 4 words (only if the phrase is extremely punchy)

## Step 3: Generate YouTube Title Options

### Click Trigger Hierarchy (from Top 10 CTR Elements, ranked by frequency in viral videos):
1. **Curiosity (56%)** — Open loops, unanswered questions
2. **Negativity (39%)** — Warnings, mistakes, "don't do this"
3. **List (28%)** — Numbers and listicles
4. **Timeliness (20%)** — Current year, trends, recent events
5. **Authority (17%)** — Expert credibility, "how pros do it"
6. **Deep Desire (14%)** — What people deeply want (save money, build a business)
7. **Time Frame (9%)** — "in 30 days," "in 5 minutes"
8. **Beginner Tips (8%)** — "for beginners," "your first"
9. **Extreme/Epic (6%)** — Dramatic, exaggerated ideas
10. **Refute Objection (5%)** — "without X," "even if Y"

### Viral Title Templates (adapted for RLS):
- "(Leading Product) is Horrible. So I Tried the Alternative" → "Rubio Monocoat Was Too Expensive. So I Made My Own."
- "Stop [Common Action] and Do This Instead" → "Stop Overpaying for Wood Finish"
- "The Secret [Authority] Doesn't Want You to Know" → "The Secret Rubio Doesn't Want You to Know"
- "I Make $X Doing Y (And Here's Exactly How)" → "I Make $30 Per Can of Furniture Oil"
- "How to [Achieve Goal] Without [Common Concern]" → "How to Finish Furniture Without Breaking the Bank"

### Title Rules for RLS:
- **Always include a curiosity element** — 56% of viral videos use it
- **Use a number when possible** — 50% of all viral videos have a number
- **Don't duplicate the thumbnail text** — title confirms and adds to the thumbnail's curiosity
- **Keep under 60 characters when possible** for full display on mobile
- **Product-hack competitor names** when relevant — "Rubio Monocoat" in the title captures their search traffic

## Step 4: Write the Description

### Description Structure:
1. **Opening paragraph (2-4 sentences):** Hook + what the viewer will learn/see. Written in brand voice. No generic "In this video..." openers.
2. **Timestamps:** Every major topic shift gets a timestamp. Use engaging labels, not boring ones. "The Costco Hot Dog Explanation" not "Business Model Discussion."
3. **"Why it matters" section:** 2-3 sentences connecting the video to the viewer's real life (saving money, increasing margins, making better choices).
4. **Product links:** All relevant RLS product URLs
5. **Social links:** Website, Instagram, Facebook, email

### Timestamp Label Rules:
- Use the most interesting/curious version of the topic
- Include key quotes in quotation marks when Zach says something memorable
- Front-load the most clickable timestamps (viewers scan these to decide if it's worth watching)

## Step 5: Generate Tags

### Tag Strategy:
- **35-40 tags total**
- First 5 tags: exact match to target keywords (most important for search)
- Next 10 tags: competitor brand names as comparison terms ("rubio monocoat alternative," "odie's oil vs")
- Next 10 tags: broad woodworking/finishing terms
- Final 10-15 tags: long-tail and related terms

### Always Include These Core Tags:
woodaddy furniture oil, rubio monocoat alternative, best furniture oil 2026, wood finish comparison, rustic lumber store, furniture oil review, save money woodworking, food safe wood finish, woodworking business, honest woodworker

## Step 6: Thumbnail Direction

Always provide a specific thumbnail direction including:
- **Image composition** (what's in the frame, where is Zach, what products are visible)
- **Facial expression** (what emotion does Zach convey)
- **Color strategy** (what colors dominate, what contrasts)
- **Text placement** (where does the thumbnail text go, what font weight/color)
- **A/B test recommendation** (always suggest 2 versions to test for 48 hours each)

---

## Output Format

Always deliver in this exact order:
1. **THUMBNAIL TEXT OPTIONS** (5 options with word count noted)
2. **YOUTUBE TITLE OPTIONS** (5 options with click triggers labeled, recommended pick noted)
3. **DESCRIPTION** (full formatted description with timestamps)
4. **TAGS** (comma-space separated)
5. **THUMBNAIL DIRECTION** (composition, expression, color, text, A/B test)
