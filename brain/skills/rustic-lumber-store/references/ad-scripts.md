# Rustic Lumber Store / Woodaddy — Ad Script System

## Ad Script Framework

When writing ad scripts for RLS/Woodaddy, every script must include:

1. **Metadata table:** Format, Duration, Audience, Funnel Stage, CTA, Platform
2. **3 hook options** (the first 3 seconds determine everything — film ALL hooks)
3. **Visual direction** (what the camera sees, where, what's in frame)
4. **Full voiceover / on-screen text** (with stage directions in brackets)
5. **End card** (product shot, CTA, tagline)

---

## Ad Formats

### Short-Form (15-30 seconds)
- Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- One concept, one CTA, no wasted frames
- Transformation clips, quick tips, controversy hooks, before/after
- Hook within first 2 seconds or it fails

### Mid-Form (30-60 seconds)
- Meta Feed ads, Instagram Feed
- Can tell a short story or make a comparison argument
- Include social proof or pricing math
- One clear CTA (sample OR full-size, never both)

### Founder DTC (60-90 seconds)
- Meta Feed, YouTube Pre-Roll
- Zach direct-to-camera in the workshop
- Origin story, comparison, credibility-building
- Can transition between teaching and selling
- Always ends with sample CTA or "shop now"

### Woodaddy Character (any duration)
- Macho Man Randy Savage energy
- Can be standalone or hybrid (Woodaddy opens, Zach delivers substance)
- Dramatic pauses, finger-pointing, bench-slapping
- Always closes with "WOODADDY." or "OHHH YEAH."

## The Woodaddy Character Voice

### Personality
- Over-the-top energy, gravelly bravado
- Deadly serious about furniture oil (humor = contrast)
- Not a cartoon — there's real substance underneath the character
- Think: Randy Savage cutting a promo on overpriced furniture finish

### Tone Rules
- Loud, confident, slightly unhinged
- Dramatic hand gestures, pointing at camera
- Every sentence lands like a catchphrase
- No hedging, no "might work for you"
- Woodaddy KNOWS this oil is the truth

### How It Works in Scripts
- Open with Woodaddy energy (grab attention)
- Can transition to real Zach for substance moments (the "drop the character" beat)
- Close with Woodaddy energy (memorable ending)
- Label dialogue as either "WOODADDY:" or "ZACH:" in scripts

### Woodaddy Catchphrases
- "WOODADDY. OUT."
- "OHHH YEAH."
- "TEN. DOLLARS."
- "[Points at camera]"
- "[Slaps bottle on bench]"

## Audience Targeting by Funnel Stage

### TOFU (Cold — Never Heard of Us)
- **Hobbyist:** Transformation videos, "watch what happens," satisfying application clips
- **DIY Renovator:** "Stop ruining your table," "one afternoon refinish," problem-solution
- **All Cold:** Founder origin story, "we made our own," shop tour

### MOFU (Warm — Aware but Not Convinced)
- **Hobbyist/Pro:** Price comparison math ($60 vs. $160), side-by-side testing, "same result"
- **All Warm:** The $10 sample pitch, customer reviews, "try it for yourself"

### BOFU (Hot — Ready to Buy or Already Visited)
- **Cart abandoners:** "Still thinking about it?" + social proof
- **Sample buyers:** "Come back for the full size" + upgrade math
- **Past purchasers:** Replenishment reminder, new product announcements

## Filming Rules (Production Notes)

- **Always shoot in the workshop.** Sawdust, tools, tables in progress. The workshop IS the brand.
- **Lavalier mic on Zach** for DTC pieces. Natural audio for transformation clips.
- **Natural lighting preferred.** The imperfection is the authenticity.
- **Batch film:** All scripts in a single 4-6 hour session. Founder DTC first (Zach freshest), then transformation B-roll, then product close-ups.
- **Every ad has ONE CTA.** Sample OR full-size. Never both.
- **Retargeting ads assume prior awareness.** Warmer tone, more direct, less educational.

---

## Hook Testing Protocol

- Film 3 hook variations for every script (same body, different first 3 seconds)
- 3-5 day test window per hook variant
- Kill rule: CPA exceeds 2× target after $30-$50 spend
- Scale rule: CPA below target for 3 consecutive days with 5+ conversions → increase 20%
- This produces 30 testable ad variations from 10 scripts

## Key Messages by Script Type

### Origin Story Scripts
Lead: "We tried every finish. Nothing was good enough for our furniture. So we made our own."
Support: Four years of development. Two-part system. Food-safe. No VOCs. One coat.

### Price Comparison Scripts
Lead: "900ml for $60. Their 900ml? $160-$200. Same result."
Support: Independent testing, YouTube comparisons, 20 five-star reviews.

### Transparency Scripts
Lead: "We make $30 per can. I'll say it out loud."
Support: Costco hot dog model, multiple revenue streams, low overhead.

### Anti-Scare-Tactic Scripts
Lead: "We're not here to scare you into buying our product."
Support: Every project has a purpose for every product. No single finish does everything.

### Small-Business Champion Scripts
Lead: "Your finish cost shouldn't eat your margins."
Support: Average woodworker at 20-30% margins. Goal: get them to 30-40%.

## CTA Hierarchy

1. **Default CTA (cold traffic):** "Try it for $10" → links to sample page
2. **Comparison CTA (warm traffic):** "Shop now" → links to main product page
3. **Upgrade CTA (sample buyers):** "Get the full size" → links to 900ml
4. **Replenishment CTA (past buyers):** "Reorder" → links to product page
5. **Never use:** "Sign up," "learn more" (too passive), or dual CTAs in one ad
