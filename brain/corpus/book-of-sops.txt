 ■ How to use this book
 Every SOP is one self-contained block: who owns it, when it fires, the exact steps, the tools, and the one sentence that tells you it is finished. When a procedure changes, change it here. The master rule above all of them: if a step can run without the founder, build it so it does. V5 adds the show. The chair is the one job that stays Brandon's through 2028; everything around the chair is written so somebody else can run it.

 // PART A · SALES & PIPELINE · TWO DOORS: THE CHAIR AND THE DIAGNOSTIC
 How a stranger becomes a paying client

 A1 Guest & lead research KID FLASH · AGENT
 PURPOSE
Build two clean, verified lists: guest candidates for the chair, and service prospects at whatever volume the Sept 25 rule allows.
 WHEN IT FIRES
Every morning, automatically. Or whenever next week shows fewer than 2 recordings booked.

 TOOLS
Apollo · OS Pipeline · the Show Bible guest matrix
 DONE WHEN
A scored guest list and a tiered prospect list are queued and logged.

 01 > Pull owner-operators at $500K–$10M , Omaha metro first, across the 7 categories and 58 owner types in the Show Bible.
 02 > Verify each: real active business, owner name + direct email, the stage jump they could speak to, one specific thing they did that is worth an episode.
 03 > Score every candidate on the 15-point Guest Score . Book at 10 or higher. Buyer-fit is 3 of the 15 points and never more.
 04 > The first four chairs are the package test. Pick them for buyer-fit on purpose: owners who sell to consumers or have a hiring problem, with a marketing budget, who are not already clients.
 05 > Honor the mix rules: no more than 2 of the same category in any 4-week window; at least 1 in 4 guests above $3M.
 06 > Dedupe against the pipeline, past outreach, current clients. Hand the scored list to Blue Beetle.

 A2 The invitation sequence BLUE BEETLE · AGENT
 PURPOSE
Turn Kid Flash's guest list into booked pre-interviews. An invitation asks for nothing and offers status. It is not a pitch.
 WHEN IT FIRES
Daily, on the scored list. First 20 out by Fri Sept 25.

 TOOLS
Email · LinkedIn · Calendar · the Guest Invitation Kit
 DONE WHEN
Replies routed; pre-interviews on the calendar; reply rate logged.

 01 > Personalize line one with the specific thing they did. Never “I came across your business.”
 02 > Run the 3-touch sequence from the Invitation Kit: email day 0, LinkedIn day 3, email day 7. Then stop.
 03 > Answer the five things a guest needs to know in plain words: how long, where, what you will ask, who watches, what they walk away with. No house vocabulary.
 04 > Route replies: yes → book the 15-minute pre-interview; not now → re-invite in 90 days; no → close out warmly.
 05 > Zero service pitches inside an invitation thread. Service outreach is a separate list at the Sept 25 volume (30% of sends through Oct 31 if the actuals show 2+ closes from outbound and ads; zero if fewer).
 06 > Log reply rate weekly. Under 15% across the first 100 sends by Oct 31 fires the rewrite tripwire.

 A3 The Diagnostic sales call BRANDON
 PURPOSE
Convert a booked call into a paid $750 Diagnostic. Door B: referrals, inbound, and the third conversation of the week.
 WHEN IT FIRES
A booked discovery call that did not come through the chair.

 TOOLS
OS Sales Script Pt 1 · Pipeline · HoneyBook
 DONE WHEN
Payment taken or invoice sent; deal at “Diagnostic Sold.”

 01 > Open / disarm: “I'm not here to pitch you a retainer.” Let them talk first.
 02 > Situation: map lead-to-customer path, volume, spend, what happens to non-buyers.
 03 > Problem awareness: “Where do you think it's breaking?” Make them name it.
 04 > Consequence: “If nothing changes in 6–12 months?” and “What is one closed job worth?”
 05 > Present the Diagnostic: $750, 48 hrs, scored teardown, 3 ranked fixes, theirs to keep. Send the sample on the call.
 06 > Commit: “Card now or invoice today?” Through Dec 31 this conversation also sells the fast-collecting products: Tournament and Tool Sprint.

 A4 Objection handling BRANDON
 PURPOSE
Turn the real objection into a yes without improvising a discount.
 WHEN IT FIRES
Any hesitation on any sales call, debrief or enrollment call.

 TOOLS
OS Sales Script Pt 2 (eleven objections, three locks)
 DONE WHEN
The objection is addressed and the close is re-attempted.

 01 > Do not argue the surface objection. Find the real lock: money, timing, or “will it be different.”
 02 > Label first, answer second, close third. A second objection is normal; it is usually the real one.
 03 > On the third objection, name it: “I'm getting the sense this is a no wearing a maybe. Which is it?”
 04 > Never invent a discount. Founder pricing is a case-study exchange with a deliverable attached, in writing.
 05 > Log the objection word for word. That language is next quarter's ad copy.

 A5 Proposal & close BRANDON
 PURPOSE
Convert a delivered Diagnostic, or a delivered Guest Package, into a retainer or product sale.
 WHEN IT FIRES
Diagnostic walkthrough done, or a package capture session finished.

 TOOLS
HoneyBook · the Diagnostic report or the capture notes · Pipeline
 DONE WHEN
Contract signed + first invoice paid, or closed-lost with a reason.

 01 > Use the three ranked fixes (Diagnostic) or what you saw in their building (capture session) as the spine.
 02 > Recommend one path. Present exactly two adjacent rungs, never three.
 03 > Price from the Offer Ladder sheet. The $750 Diagnostic credits toward month one. The package does not credit; it is a finished product.
 04 > Send same day via HoneyBook; contract + first invoice attached. Engine 4-month minimum, System 6-month.
 05 > If the work they need is strategy above production, it is Kelly's. Make the introduction the same day.
 06 > Follow up on a set cadence until signed or a clear no.

 A6 Pipeline management BRANDON · WATCHTOWER ASSISTS
 PURPOSE
Keep an accurate, current view of every deal and every chair.
 WHEN IT FIRES
Continuous; reviewed every Friday.

 TOOLS
OS Pipeline + Command dashboard
 DONE WHEN
Pipeline reflects reality and the Friday Five is logged.

 01 > Every guest enters Pipeline at “Invited” and moves: Pre-interview → Recorded → Published → Debrief held → Package / Engine / Alumni.
 02 > Tag every dollar by door: chair-sourced , Diagnostic door, cohort, base expansion.
 03 > Track chair-sourced cash net of production against the $15,000 Dec 31 line.
 04 > Any Friday with fewer than 2 recordings booked for next week: Blue Beetle doubles sends and Brandon makes 5 personal asks that day.
 05 > Log the 3 conversations a week. No log, no week.

 A7 The debrief call NEW BRANDON
 PURPOSE
Turn a guest whose episode is already live into a paid $2,500 cut. Where Lane 1 lives.
 WHEN IT FIRES
A guest accepts the publish-day invitation (SOP E5).

 TOOLS
OS Sales Script Pt 3 · the three-cut one-pager · HoneyBook
 DONE WHEN
Payment taken or invoice sent and the capture session booked; or a clean no, logged, with promotion unchanged.

 01 > Sentence one is the wall: the episode gets the same push whether or not you ever do business.
 02 > Reaction → the louder problem (owners, customers, or hiring) → what it is costing → the three cuts → commit.
 03 > They pick the cut. You never choose it for them. Above $3M, ask who else on their team should be in the conversation.
 04 > “Card now or invoice today?” Book the 45-minute capture session before the call ends.
 05 > Ask for one referral name for the chair, whatever the outcome.
 06 > Log verbatim. Zero paid packages from the first four buyer-fit debriefs by Nov 6 fires the JSA re-trial.
