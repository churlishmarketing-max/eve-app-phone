 // 03 · STANDING RULES · THE NON-NEGOTIABLES
 The rules that never move

 RULE 01
 Ad spend is always separate
 $500/mo minimum. Never bundled into a retainer number, paid directly to the platform, never marked up.

 RULE 02
 Never discount month one
 The first-three price on the Tournament and the guest package is a case-study exchange with a deliverable attached, framed that way in writing. Not a markdown.

 RULE 03
 50% upfront on one-offs
 All one-offs and sprints collect half before work starts. PIF incentives are bonus deliverables, never price cuts.

 RULE 04
 6% annual uplift
 Every retainer agreement carries it. Pricing keeps pace without a renegotiation.

 RULE 05 · NEW
 Nobody pays for a chair
 No offer before, during, or on the day of a recording. Guests who never buy get the same edit and the same promotion. Clients and sponsors are disclosed on air.

 RULE 06 · NEW
 Meta ads have three jobs
 Amplify clips that already won. Fill the cohort, warm audiences only. Retarget episode viewers with package proof. Cold lead gen for Churlish is off.
