 // 00 · WHAT ADS ARE FOR NOW · V5 · CHURLISH'S OWN SPEND
 Meta has three jobs

 Cold lead generation for Churlish services is off. Client campaigns are untouched: this section governs Churlish's own spend only. $500/mo floor, item-level approval before anything goes live, the diagnostic law decides scale or kill.
 JOB 01
 Amplify what already won
 A clip earns spend only after it beats the channel organically. Starting rule until Cassandra Cain has tape to read: top 2 of the last 10 shorts by 3-second hold and shares. Money follows proof, never the other way round.

 JOB 02
 Fill the cohort , warm only
 Episode viewers and the email list. Runs only while a deposit or enrollment window is open (Oct 19 → Nov 7, then Nov 10 → start). One CTA: the refundable $250 seat.

 JOB 03
 Package proof, retargeted
 Once a Guest Package case study exists (day-45 receipt, with the exchange addendum signed), show it to people who watched that guest's episode. Owners in the guest's trade first.

 ★ What never runs
 No ad recruits guests: the chair is filled by personal invitation. No ad uses a guest's clip to sell Churlish services without separate written permission (release §4). No cold traffic to the cohort. No ad without a concrete offer behind the CTA.

 // 01 · THE DNA · SIX BEATS, FIXED ORDER
 The call-out formula

 The sequence never changes; the sections compress or expand with format length. For 15s and statics, beats 2–4 compress into a single move. If a script does not follow this arc, it usually is not ours.

 // 02 · SCRIPT TEMPLATES · BY LENGTH
 Four formats, one arc

 :15 15-second script 35–45 WORDS
 PLATFORMS
IG/FB Reels · YouTube Shorts · TikTok
 RULE
The hook must work with sound OFF. Every sentence speakable in one breath.

 01 > [HOOK — on-screen text] One bold sentence. 3 seconds.
 02 > [PAIN + PIVOT] 1–2 sentences. 5 seconds.
 03 > [PROOF + OFFER] One sentence: specific result, then what they get. 4 seconds.
 04 > [CTA — text + voiceover] One action. 3 seconds.

 :30 30-second script — the workhorse 70–90 WORDS
 PLATFORMS
Meta feed · YouTube pre-roll · CTV
 RULE
The call-out carries at least one specific number: a dollar amount, a percentage, or a timeframe.

 01 > [HOOK] 1–2 sentences · 3–5s.
 02 > [CALL-OUT] 2–3 sentences · 8–10s.
 03 > [PROOF] 1–2 sentences · 5–7s.
 04 > [OFFER + CTA] 2 sentences · 8–10s.

 :60 60-second script 140–170 WORDS
 PLATFORMS
Meta · YouTube · CTV · long-form social
 RULE
Math-breakdown technique in the call-out: walk their actual numbers until they feel the gap.

 01 > [HOOK] 3–5s.
 02 > [CALL-OUT] 4–6 sentences · 15–20s.
 03 > [PIVOT] 5–7s.
 04 > [PROOF] a specific case study · 10–12s.
 05 > [OFFER] 8–10s.
 06 > [CTA] 5s.

 :90+ 90-second to 2-minute — VSL-style 200–300 WORDS
 PLATFORMS
YouTube mid-roll · long-form social · landing page
 RULE
The story open earns the length.

 01 > [HOOK] 5s.
 02 > [STORY OPEN] personal or client story · 15–20s.
 03 > [CALL-OUT] pain with data · 20–30s.
 04 > [PIVOT] 10–15s.
 05 > [PROOF] before/after and timeline · 15–20s.
 06 > [OFFER + CTA] one action; close the loop the hook opened.

 // 03 · SHOW TEMPLATES · NEW
 Three ads the show makes possible

 New in V5. Each is the call-out arc wearing different clothes: the guest supplies the hook and the proof, so the ad never has to claim anything.
 S·1 The clip amplifier JOB 01 · 20–45s
 PLATFORMS
Reels · Shorts · Meta feed
 RULE
Do not rewrite a clip that already won. Add one text hook and one end card. Nothing else.

 01 > [HOOK — text over the first frame] The number jump + the trade: “$400K to $2.1M. Roofing. Here's mistake #1.”
 02 > [THE CLIP] Untouched. The guest is the call-out, the pivot and the proof.
 03 > [END CARD] “Full episode + the one-page breakdown.” One link.
 04 > [CTA] “Get the one-page breakdown of this episode, free.”

 S·2 The cohort filler JOB 02 · 30–60s
 PLATFORMS
Meta, warm audiences only
 RULE
The instructors are the headline, not Brandon. Use their faces and their numbers.

 01 > [HOOK] If/then conditional: “If you've been stuck between $300K and $500K for two years, this is who got out.”
 02 > [CALL-OUT] The stage, in their words, from the episodes.
 03 > [PROOF] Four owners, four number jumps, five seconds each.
 04 > [OFFER] Eight weeks. Ten seats. Taught by them. Starts [DATE] .
 05 > [CTA] “Hold a seat with $250. It comes straight back if you change your mind.”

 S·3 The package proof JOB 03 · 30s
 PLATFORMS
Meta retargeting: viewers of that guest's episode
 RULE
Runs only with a day-45 receipt and a signed exchange addendum.

 01 > [HOOK] Specific result: “ [GUEST] needed two techs. [N] applied in three weeks.”
 02 > [PIVOT] “It was one 45-minute shoot in his shop.”
 03 > [PROOF] Ten seconds of the actual cut.
 04 > [OFFER + CTA] “See exactly what one session made for [GUEST] .” Link to the case study, never to a booking page.

 ▲ Production standard — every script ships as
 A 9:16 , a 1:1 , and a 6-second cutdown for retargeting, plus three opening-frame variants so the ad keeps testing as it scales. Runners-up stay on the bench and rotate in when frequency climbs past 2.5.

 // 04 · THE HOOK LIBRARY · 8 FORMULAS
 Eight ways to stop a thumb

 If the hook fails, nothing else matters.
 # Formula Structure Example
 01 Direct callout “You're [specific thing] and [uncomfortable consequence].” “You've got 47 five-star reviews and you're still competing on price.”
 02 Uncomfortable truth “[Thing everyone believes] is [costing them].” “Your marketing agency is the most expensive employee you'll never fire.”
 03 Specific result “[Number]. [Short context].” “47 leads in 21 days. Here's exactly what we did.”
 04 Provocative question One they cannot scroll past. Never yes/no. “How many customers did you lose this month because they couldn't find you?”
 05 “Stop doing this” “Stop [thing that's hurting them].” “Stop competing on price. You're better than that.”
 06 If/then conditional “If you're [situation], [what's actually happening].” “If your best channel is word of mouth, you don't have marketing. You have luck.”
 07 Pattern interrupt A visual break the feed does not expect. Pair with another formula. White text on black, nothing else: “Your agency won't tell you this.”
 08 Social proof stack “[Name] did [result]. [Name] did [result]. Here's what they have in common.” On the show this writes itself: three guests, three jumps, one mistake they shared.
 THE WRITING RULES
 Write 10, keep 3
 Specificity beats cleverness. The hook's only job is to earn the next 5 seconds. Read it out loud; if you stumble, the viewer scrolls. Never open with the brand name.

 A / B DISCIPLINE
 One formula per variant
 Build 3–5 variants from different formulas for the same offer and avatar, and label each with its formula, so the scoreboard tells you which trigger wins, not just which sentence.

 // 05 · CTA LAW · ENFORCED ON EVERY AD, EVERY CLIENT, NO EXCEPTIONS
 The CTA standards

 PROPERTY 1
 Benefit- led
 Names what they GET, not what they do.

 PROPERTY 2
 Urgency- loaded
 A reason to act now: scarcity, deadline, qualification.

 PROPERTY 3
 Qualifier- baked
 Implies who it is for; filters bad-fit clicks.

 PROPERTY 4
 Effort- light
 Sounds easy and valuable, not like work.

 ★ Banned — these never ship, even if the client signed off
 Book a call with me Schedule a call Learn more Click here Click here to book Want to chat? Let's talk Hop on a call Call me to learn more Get in touch Contact us today Sign up
 They frame the CTA as work for the prospect, carry zero benefit signal, no urgency, no qualifier. Auto-replace with an approved pattern and explain the swap.

 Door The one approved CTA
 Client ads (call-out) Approved patterns from the V4 table, unchanged: “Let's map out your game plan — grab a free call today.” · “See exactly how [specific result] happens.”
 The show, every episode “Get the one-page breakdown of this episode, free.” The same CTA every episode. One link, one place.
 The cohort “Hold a seat with $250. It comes straight back if you change your mind.”
 Package proof “See exactly what one session made for [GUEST] .”
 Diagnostic (Door B) “Find out exactly what's broken and what it's costing you. $750, 48 hours.”
 ▲ Hard rules
 One CTA per ad , never two. The CTA must match a concrete offer. It appears at the end AND in the headline/copy, phrased differently. Test 2–3 CTA variants per campaign, all from the approved patterns.
