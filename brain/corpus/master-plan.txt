 // 01 · THESIS · THE WHOLE GAME
 The strategic bet

 $ CAT THE_BET.TXT
 The first AI-operated story studio in the Midwest
 Unchanged. Churlish wins the next four years not by selling marketing deliverables — that layer is commoditizing under every agency's feet, including ours — but by pairing cinema-grade story on the front end with an autonomous operating system on the back end, sold as outcomes at clear prices. We eat our own delivery cost with AI before a competitor uses AI to eat our price.

 ★ What v5 adds to the bet
 AI makes cold outreach cheaper for everyone, which makes it worth less for everyone. An invitation is a different animal: it asks for nothing and offers status. The owners Churlish most wants will not answer a pitch. They will answer an invitation. So the show becomes the room where the relationship starts, the fleet does everything around the chair, and the score that runs everything stays the same: how much of this was delivered without Brandon in the room?

 No offer before, during, or on the day of a recording. Guests who never buy get the same edit and the same promotion. Clients and sponsors are disclosed on air.

 RULE 06 · NEW

 Amplify clips that already won. Fill the cohort, warm audiences only. Retarget episode viewers with package proof. Cold lead gen for Churlish is off.
