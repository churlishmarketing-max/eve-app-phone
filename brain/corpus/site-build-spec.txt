 // 01 · THE JOB · SCOPE IS CAPPED BEFORE CRAFT STARTS
 One page. One form. Nothing else until Oct 4.

 WHAT THE PAGE IS FOR
 Get the right owner to apply
 It answers four questions in plain words: what the show is, why it exists, how you get on it, how you apply. A second job rides along: capture the email of an owner who is not ready for the chair.

 WHAT IS NOT IN SCOPE
 Everything else
 No blog, no episode pages, no guest portal, no cohort checkout, no sponsor page, no Churlish service links. A full build eats the five days that belong to invitations. Week two and later.

 THE STACK
 Lovable · Supabase · Resend
 Lovable builds the page. Applications and emails land in Supabase. Resend notifies hello@churlishmedia.com on every application. Domain points when Doctor Mid-Nite clears it.

 ★ Two house laws that bind this page
 Plain words. A guest never reads "Authority Engine," "debrief" or "Breakthrough Arc." CTA law. No "Learn more," "Sign up," "Get in touch" or "Contact us" anywhere on the page. Every button names what the person gets. And nothing on this page sells a Churlish service: the wall is public here.

 // 03 · THE BUILD PROMPT
 Paste this into Lovable

 Attach this PDF or the paste file with it. The prompt carries the rules; the spec carries the words.
 Build a one-page, mobile-first marketing site for an interview show called High Level Pros.
Single route ("/") with anchor navigation: What it is · Why · The chair · Apply.
Do not add pages, a blog, a login, or a CMS. One page and one application form is the whole scope.

COPY: use the copy in the attached spec exactly as written, section S1 through S10, in that order.
Do not rewrite it, do not add marketing phrases, do not add testimonials, stats or logos that are
not in the spec. If a section needs content that is not provided, leave it out.

LOOK: near-black background (#0A0A0A), cream text (#EDEAE3), muted gray body (#9B968D), one accent
red (#F11B28) used sparingly for the primary button and section numbers. Headlines in Barlow
Condensed 900, uppercase, tight leading. Labels and small text in JetBrains Mono, letter-spaced.
Thin 1px borders (#252525), square corners, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no emoji, no stock
photos. Image slots take real photography supplied later; use a flat dark placeholder until then.

DATA (Supabase): table `applications` (id, created_at, name, business, city, one_sentence,
revenue_band, years_owner, team_size, stuck_number, what_moved_it, teach_15, numbers_on_camera,
referred_by, status default 'new'). Table `subscribers` (id, created_at, email unique, source).
Row-level security on; inserts only from the public site. Add a hidden honeypot field to both forms.

EMAIL (Resend): on each application insert, send a plain-text notification with every field to
hello@churlishmedia.com. No auto-reply beyond the on-page confirmation line.

BEHAVIOUR: client-side validation with plain error text; the confirmation line replaces the form
after submit; YouTube embeds lazy-load; every button has a visible focus state; WCAG AA contrast.
Fire analytics events: apply_start, apply_submit, email_submit, episode_click.

SEO / AI ANSWERS: title "High Level Pros · Midwest owners, real numbers". Meta description: the S1
sub line. Add schema.org PodcastSeries and an FAQPage built from S5 (the five plain answers).
Open Graph image slot, 1200x630.

ONE SECTION IS BUILT BUT HIDDEN: "The Next Stage" (a cohort deposit block) behind a single
