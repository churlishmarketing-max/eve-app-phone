 // 02 · THE METHOD
 The Authority Engine — how we think

 The whole philosophy in one idea: authority is earned through proof, not claimed through adjectives. Most businesses advertise by listing how long they've existed and how "trusted" they are. We lead with the customer's problem, stack real proof, then make an offer.

 ★ The call-out structure · our default ad shape
 If a script doesn't follow this arc, it usually isn't ours. Two phrases carry the brand: "Proof before promise" and "Red is a scalpel, not a paintbrush."  ▲ Why the chair, in one paragraph
 A straight sales call costs about one Brandon-hour and leaves notes. A fit guest costs about two and a half hours and leaves an owner who told us their numbers, 12 published pieces, a warm relationship and a referral surface. The chair loses on closes per hour and wins on everything else. So the weekly floor stays at three conversations: two chairs plus one straight sales conversation. Through Dec 31 that third conversation sells the fast-collecting products: Diagnostic, Tournament, Tool Sprint.

 The rules that never move
 RULE 01
 Ad spend is always separate
 $500/mo minimum. Never bundled into a retainer number, paid directly to the platform, never marked up.

 RULE 02
 Never discount month one
 The first-three price on the Tournament and the guest package is a case-study exchange with a deliverable attached, framed that way in writing. Not a markdown.

 RULE 03
 50% upfront on one-offs
 All one-offs and sprints collect half before work starts. PIF incentives are bonus deliverables, never price cuts.

 6% annual uplift
 Every retainer agreement carries it. Pricing keeps pace without a renegotiation.

 RULE 05 · NEW
 Nobody pays for a chair
 No offer before, during, or on the day of a recording. Guests who never buy get the same edit and the same promotion. Clients and sponsors are disclosed on air.

 RULE 06 · NEW
 Meta ads have three jobs
 Amplify clips that already won. Fill the cohort, warm audiences only. Retarget episode viewers with package proof. Cold lead gen for Churlish is off.

 // 07 · SALES
 How we sell — three conversations a week

 CONVERSATION 1 + 2
 The chair
 Two recordings a week. A recording is a conversation you cannot build your way out of. Nothing is sold in the room. The invitation to talk rides with the published episode, and that talk (the debrief) is where the $2,500 cut gets offered.

 CONVERSATION 3
 The straight one
 Referrals, inbound, the existing pipeline. Runs the Diagnostic call script: NEPQ spine, Voss handwork, "card now, or invoice today?" Sells the $750 Diagnostic, the Tournament, the Tool Sprint.

 THE METHOD
 NEPQ + Voss
 We ask, we don't pitch. They talk 70%. Every hesitation is one of three locks: money, timing, or "will it be different." Find the lock. Never the discount.

 ★ The editorial wall · non-negotiable
 Guests are booked on the Guest Score. Buyer-fit is 3 of 15 points and can never be the reason someone is booked.
 No offer of any kind before, during, or on the day of a recording. The invitation to talk arrives with the published episode.
 A guest who never buys gets the same edit, the same packaging, the same promotion.
 If a guest is a current client or a sponsor, the episode says so on air and in the description.
 Nobody pays to sit in the chair. Ever.
